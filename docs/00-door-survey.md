# Phase 0: Door Survey Sheet

Lock: **Kwikset deadbolt with keyed exterior cylinder** (user-identified as 663; likely 660 family).

**Photos are optional.** If you can't take them, the fit-test coupons described in PLAN.md section 6 cover the critical dimensions.

**No tools needed:** measurements can be taken from photos. Put a reference object of known size flat against the same surface you're photographing, and shoot straight-on from about 30 cm (not at an angle, not zoomed). Good references:
- a credit/debit card or ID card: 85.6 x 54.0 mm (cover the numbers)
- a US quarter: 24.26 mm diameter
- a US dollar bill: 156 x 66 mm

Printed rulers are less reliable, because printers rescale. For depth (how far things stick out), take a side-on photo with the card standing on edge next to the part.

## Photos to take
1. Interior side of the door, full view, showing deadbolt, handle and surroundings.
2. Thumb turn, straight-on from the front.
3. Thumb turn, side view (shows how far it sticks out from the plate).
4. Thumb turn **removed** (unscrew the 2 screws): the plate and the exposed spindle/tailpiece, straight-on.
5. Same, from the side, showing how far the spindle protrudes from the plate.
6. The thumb turn's back, showing how it couples to the spindle.
7. The 2 screws, next to a ruler.
8. Any brand or model markings (Kwikset, Schlage, Defiant, etc.) on the thumb turn, plate or latch edge.
9. Handle (knob or lever) and its position relative to the deadbolt, side view.

## Torque measurement
**No scale?** Turn the thumb turn so its tip points sideways (horizontal), hang a plastic bag from the tip with string, and pour in water slowly until it turns. 1 litre of water is about 1 kg. Torque (N·m) = kg x 9.81 x distance from the centre to the string (m), measured from a photo.

**With a luggage scale:**
1. With the thumb turn reattached, hook a luggage scale to the end of the thumb turn (or a stick clamped to it), a known distance from the spindle centre.
2. Pull perpendicular to the lever arm and record the force at the moment it starts to move, and while it moves.
3. Torque (N·m) = force (N) x distance (m). (1 kg of force is about 9.81 N.)
4. Repeat 5 times each for lock and unlock, with the door open, then closed and unlatched, then pushed hard against the frame (worst case).

## Intent-detection spike (free, uses a phone)
1. Install **phyphox** (free) and open its accelerometer experiment.
2. Tape the phone to the interior handle (or flat on the door near it).
3. Record 10 trials each of: turning the exterior handle, pushing the door open, knocking, someone leaning on the door, door slamming nearby.
4. Export the data. The goal is to see whether a handle turn has a signature that is distinct and early enough.
